## Purpose

Enable independently useful deliveries to build and publish without unnecessarily stopping authorized independent development, while preserving source identity, recovery, truthful evidence and publication obligations.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Bounded work-ahead preserves authority and elapsed work
Cadence SHALL retain one active delivery and one authorized future implementation scope, with isolated source/output ownership, explicit dependencies and existing KBD approval authority. Promotion MUST require predecessor local success, resolved required hooks/review and repaired-base reconciliation; elapsed work SHALL begin at the original firstWorkAt. Unresolved children MUST retain return criteria, elapsed time and publication debt.

#### Scenario: Future work begins during a frozen build
- **WHEN** completed candidate A builds and independent scope B is explicitly admitted
- **THEN** B can be implemented in isolated roots without another active KBD owner; promotion retains B's original work time

#### Scenario: A predecessor fails
- **WHEN** A fails its build or operation
- **THEN** dependent promotion and publication are blocked, repair receives contested-resource priority, and only independently authorized disjoint editing can continue

### Requirement: Feature-operation readiness survives scope splitting
Planning, dispatch and admission SHALL preserve the promised capability, procedure, source driver or direct command sequence, target/dependency evidence level, prerequisites, resource isolation and side-effect authority. A missing procedure MUST name an approved creation task before admission. Ready/freeze MUST require that source work complete; generated entrypoints SHALL be resolved after their build. Operation SHALL invoke the actual project entrypoint or supported direct production interface; merely registering a test-suite runner MUST NOT substitute for feature operation. Substitute-backed execution of that entrypoint SHALL support only its explicitly approved limited claim. Substitutes MUST NOT satisfy an approved real-service claim, and missing work MUST NOT silently create a task or external-effect authority.

#### Scenario: A phase split loses its operation
- **WHEN** a proposed child increment has no inherited or explicitly revised feature-operation contract
- **THEN** admission reports the missing contract and owning scope decision before execution, without silently adding work

#### Scenario: The entrypoint is generated
- **WHEN** the approved direct CLI operation uses a build-produced file
- **THEN** ready checks completed source and its declared generation recipe; the operation checks the generated file after build

### Requirement: Candidates and receipts preserve immutable provenance
Every frozen candidate SHALL identify complete scope, source/runtime assets, build recipe, execution ownership and feature operation. Actual release input changes MUST invalidate applicability of earlier receipts without deleting history. Successful equivalent external receipts SHALL be adoptable without rebuilding, only with matching source/command/platform provenance, artifact bytes and required operation evidence. Receipt identity changes MUST conflict.

#### Scenario: A direct build already succeeded
- **WHEN** its immutable evidence matches the frozen candidate and approved checkpoint contract
- **THEN** Cadence adopts that evidence without executing a duplicate build; launch alone cannot satisfy new-function operation

### Requirement: Long operations do not monopolize metadata ownership
Cadence SHALL keep state mutations short while durable attempts execute outside the metadata mutex. Concurrent status and allowed independent mutations SHALL remain usable. Reconciliation MUST check current entity/source/ownership identity and MUST NOT overwrite newer state with an old snapshot. Cooperating runs SHALL reserve common physical output resources across run roots.

#### Scenario: Two runs target the same output
- **WHEN** separate state roots request one occupied physical target
- **THEN** only its recorded owner executes and the other reports the resource conflict without blocking unrelated status

### Requirement: Recovery retains uncertain effects and command identity
Repeated command ID and identical request SHALL return the recorded outcome; changed request content MUST conflict. Unknown launches, reused process identities, cancellation uncertainty and non-idempotent hook effects MUST remain unresolved until observed reconciliation. Cancellation SHALL address the owned process tree, not a guessed process. Known successful effects MUST NOT be replayed automatically.

#### Scenario: Launch acknowledgement is lost
- **WHEN** a resume finds dispatch intent but no confirmed launch/result
- **THEN** it reconciles available identity evidence and reports unknown if unresolved instead of starting a duplicate

### Requirement: Publication obligations remain independently attributable
Cadence SHALL expose manual, successful-count, UTC-interval and either-trigger opportunities through invoked evaluation without a daemon. Eligible, skipped-no-change and held-not-ready outcomes SHALL be explicit. Due obligations MUST preserve their original threshold/time, policy and candidate coverage across later deliveries, children and configuration changes. Completion SHALL clear only matching obligations. One full release and one pending candidate SHALL be the initial capacity; truthful completion recording MUST remain possible under backpressure.

#### Scenario: An older release finishes after another becomes due
- **WHEN** obligation A completes while B is owed
- **THEN** A's receipts resolve A and B remains due with its original date and coverage

#### Scenario: A scheduled window has no meaningful candidate
- **WHEN** invoked evaluation finds only bookkeeping or incomplete functionality
- **THEN** it records skipped-no-change or held-not-ready without creating delivery credit or discarding existing debt

### Requirement: External publication is evidence-based and capability-limited
External attempts SHALL record immutable candidate/platform inputs, stable correlation, dispatch intent and observed results. Artifacts SHALL publish incrementally while prior working links remain for pending targets. Full publication SHALL require all configured artifacts, metadata and site evidence; installed acceptance SHALL remain separate. Unsupported exact-source dispatch or target-wide serialization MUST produce an explicit blocked capability and owning follow-up. Automatic coalescing and retargeting running attempts MUST be prohibited.

#### Scenario: The current publisher requires a release branch version
- **WHEN** its release remains in flight
- **THEN** version-changing branch promotion is protected while independent development continues; local reservations are not claimed to fence unrelated external publishers

#### Scenario: A pending candidate is replaced
- **WHEN** the operator explicitly authorizes replacement before dispatch
- **THEN** the new record retains covered scope, source compatibility and original obligation dates; no running attempt is retargeted

### Requirement: Migration and distribution preserve owned state
Explicit backed-up v3 migration SHALL preserve v1/v2 histories, identities, unresolved outcomes and unknown observations while excluding old mutators. Full and mini SHALL distribute identical shared payloads using existing ownership-preserving routes, with pack-specific adapters kept separate. User-authored conflicts and unavailable native execution MUST remain visible and MUST NOT be reported as successful installation or operation.

#### Scenario: An old run lacks publication job evidence
- **WHEN** it migrates with a known due obligation
- **THEN** the debt is preserved without inventing job identities, timestamps or successful effects

### Requirement: Learning distinguishes evidence from activity
Reports SHALL distinguish tasks/changes/phases, gross/reopened/net progress, usable outcomes, work-ahead and child time, overlap, timing coverage, repeated-build causes and publication/acceptance states. Optimization MUST honor configured comparable minimum samples and leave missing time unknown. Production implementation SHALL finish before the single complete operation boundary; only failed scenarios SHALL repeat after repair.

#### Scenario: More tasks were recorded with incomplete timing
- **WHEN** fewer than the configured eligible samples exist
- **THEN** reporting states insufficient velocity evidence rather than claiming productivity improvement
