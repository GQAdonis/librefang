# Dot calls, meetings and media roadmap

**Status:** proposed implementation and qualification contract. The [voice addendum](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1NSZwAcsBF-1_BTXaORezJzQZ_CpCIQHn/view) defines a first owner-to-agent WebRTC call with VC01–VC17 and eight proposed MVP gates. Later user requests expand the roadmap to online meeting attendance, in-person mobile companionship, LiveKit deployments, video avatars, authorized voice/likeness creation and outbound calls. These later requests do not mean any integration, model partnership, platform bot admission or legal qualification already exists. No actual meeting or phone call is authorized by this document.

## Milestones and media authority

| Milestone | Scope and admission | Required evidence before availability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| M1 owner call | One authenticated owner calls the dot in their existing conversation, foreground audio-only. HTTPS/WSS signaling binds owner, tenant, dot, conversation, call ID, processing route and generation. WebRTC uses ICE/STUN and authenticated TURN, DTLS-SRTP; show endpoint and plaintext processors. | Call start/end/mute/captions/barge-in/text continuity, selected local/cloud STT/LLM/TTS, transport and privacy gates VC01–VC17. On-device-only mode has no external traffic and must not claim remote WebRTC. Recording off by default. No PSTN or third-party dial in M1. |
| M2 online meeting participant | The dot joins an admitted owner-controlled room or a named platform only through that platform's supported invitation/bot integration. Each platform adapter passes its own contract matrix. |

| M5 outbound calls | Owner-authorized phone call to a verified recipient, with truthful AI identification, bounded purpose and spend/duration, required human review and live takeover. Emergency and high-impact calls excluded until separately approved and qualified. No real calls in fixture tests. |

**One authority rule applies to all modes.** The media worker handles tracks, transcript and turn events; it shares the coordinator's owner/task binding and has no connector effect credentials. Participant utterances, provisional ASR, a recognized voice or a joined call cannot confer owner authority. The host, using UAR admission, evaluates exact actions and required authenticated owner confirmation. The trusted coordinator may prepare a proposal; a quality reviewer cannot consent for the user. A changed destination, payload, speaker claim or scope invalidates the old approval.

## Call and meeting lifecycle

Track separate facts: signaling connected, ICE path, microphone permission, capture active, remote audio received, speech processor ready, agent response ready and external task state. A socket alone does not imply listening or answer readiness. Persist owner/conversation/call/meeting IDs, session generation, processing and consent revisions, event sequence, start/end reason and redacted receipts; omit raw SDP/candidates, credentials and media from the ordinary task ledger.

State: `requested -> admission_pending -> connecting -> active -> paused/reconnecting -> active | failed -> ended`. `end`, consent withdrawal, host removal or generation loss fences capture and new proposals immediately. Ending a call does not undo already authorized external effects; show outstanding tasks and offer explicit cancellation. Reconnect only the original logical call/participant, deduplicate signaling/transcript events and do not replay provider mutations. Proposed M1 budget from the addendum: setup expires after 30 seconds; at most two ICE restarts in 20 seconds; capture pauses during reconnect and requires visible resume. Later platform adapters must declare their own tested limits.

Transcript segments carry `{call_id, conversation_id, event_sequence, speaker_label, speaker_confidence, start/end, provisional/final, source_track, processing_route, consent_revision}`. Speaker attribution is uncertain unless a separate method is consented and qualified; do not silently use biometrics as authentication. Preserve what speech was actually played separately from generated text. Echo cancellation and self-audio suppression must prevent the dot from treating its own output as a new instruction. Barge-in stops playback and discards obsolete queued output; interrupted tool proposals remain subject to the current action revision.

Separate **ephemeral processing**, **saved transcript**, **saved summary**, **raw audio recording**, **avatar training material** and **operational audit receipt** as different data classes. A live processing consent is not recording or training consent. Recording and cloning are off by default; opting in names subjects, destination, use, retention and revocation. Show visible state continuously, support pause/mute/leave and export/delete where eligible, disclose backup and provider retention limits and prevent use of unconsented third-party voices or likenesses. Audio/video gateways that terminate WebRTC can read plaintext; TURN normally relays encrypted packets without becoming an endpoint. State actual media processors and downstream cloud recipients, including separate STT, model, TTS, avatar and analytics services. No silent local-to-cloud fallback.

For group answers, retrieval uses the authorized meeting/project scope, with citations and observation times. The dot must avoid disclosing private owner memory or another project merely because someone in the room asks. Floor policy: default silent listener, respond when addressed or on owner push-to-talk; require an explicit opt-in for autonomous intervention and rate-limit it. Addressed questions can be answered only when the relevant consent, recipient scope and evidence allow it. Wrong-speaker commands and background speech create proposals at most, never external actions.

## Fictional meeting acceptance fixture

Use an invented royalty-report data set with fictional people, parcels and amounts. No client records, attendee names, financial values or private transcript may enter public issue bodies or test fixtures. The dot receives authorized report definitions and meeting notes and must:

1. Answer grounded questions with source/time citations and uncertainty, including the requirement that **decimal interest appears on all reports**.
2. Prepare Purchases views grouped by **Landman / Month**, **County / Month**, and **Month / County**, with month derived from **Effective Date**. Show subtotals and totals; flag unresolved decimal precision and subtotal scope rather than guessing.
3. Prepare a closing-letter layout with deductions and net wire, marking the result as a proposal until a human verifies financial semantics. No transfer or external financial action is permitted.
4. Capture candidate decisions and action items with speaker label/confidence, time and source segment. Ask the owner to confirm durable commitments and any repository follow-up. A meeting participant's suggestion is not owner instruction.
5. Prepare an exact repository issue for a confirmed follow-up, check for duplicates, submit only after authorized admission, read it back and store the URL/receipt. On unknown submission, reconcile rather than create another issue.

Adversarial tests: noisy room, overlapping voices, echo/self-audio, wrong-speaker directive, malicious retrieved document, private-data disclosure request, interrupted utterance, participant or host consent withdrawal, microphone revocation, network switch, offline mobile, app suspension and duplicate reconnect callbacks. Assert zero unauthorized effects, no hidden capture, correct participant count, accurate partial/unknown states, deterministic task revisions and recoverable receipts. These tests are necessary for M2/M3 and complement the eight proposed M1 gates; numerical latency/reliability targets require recorded hardware, language, network and model versions.

## Future adapter decisions to qualify

Select a first certified client and mobile device matrix, self-hosted TURN/LiveKit packaging, allowed hosted endpoints, speech stack, meeting platform, background policy, avatar provider and telecom provider only after contract and source review. A claimed vendor partnership, compatibility label or model name is not an integration test. For each adapter capture supported APIs, account permissions, admission/consent policy, quota, data destination and retention, failure/reconnect semantics, exact receipt strength, licensing and a reproducible qualification fixture. Keep unsupported routes disabled and report why.

In particular, [MiniMax-H3's published community license](https://huggingface.co/MiniMaxAI/MiniMax-H3/blob/main/LICENSE) contains territory exclusions for the USA, EU, UK and Republic of Korea and invites a separate license for excluded territory use. Do not describe those weights as unrestricted open source or select them as a US default. Vendor partnership claims and Xiaomi MiMo 2.6 availability remain unverified here; model qualification must record exact model/version, license, region, API and permission evidence before enablement.
